# Privacy policy: Little Muslim (working name)

Last updated: [DATE OF FIRST RELEASE]

Little Muslim is a free app that helps children aged 8 to 12 learn the Quran,
duas and hadith. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. This policy
explains the little information the app handles.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for a name, email, age, location, contacts,
photos or microphone, and we do not receive any information about who uses it.

## What stays on the phone

These are saved only on the device and never sent to us:

- Which surah rounds and games have been finished (the mosaic)
- Which days the app was used this week
- How many minutes the app was used each day, for the parent's time limit
- Settings chosen in the parent area
- Recitation audio the user chose to save for offline listening

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## Internet connections the app makes

The app works offline. When a connection is available it makes only these
requests, without sending any personal information:

| What | Sent to | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check for updated Quran, hadith and dua text | Supabase Storage (our content host) | Keep translations current |
| Download recitation audio | Islamic Network CDN (alquran.cloud) | Play and save recitations |

Like any internet request, these services can see the device's IP address and
basic technical details. We do not ask them for this information and do not
receive it. Their own privacy terms apply: [Supabase privacy policy URL],
[AlQuran.cloud terms](https://alquran.cloud/terms-and-conditions).

## Children

The app is made for children. It follows the Google Play Families Policy: no
advertising ID, no location, no personal data collection, no ads and no
links out of the app without a parent's check. The parent area is behind an
adult question.
